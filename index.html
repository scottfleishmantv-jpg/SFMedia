About Me

I am an Emmy Award-winning storyteller and Director of Social Media with 20 years across broadcast journalism and digital media. Since 2023, I have built the social and video operation for the Nucar Family of Dealerships from scratch, leading a small remote team producing content for 25+ dealerships, work that has generated more than 28 million organic views across Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram. Before that came 15 years in television news as a sports anchor and reporter, honored with a New England Emmy and five Edward R. Murrow Awards.
